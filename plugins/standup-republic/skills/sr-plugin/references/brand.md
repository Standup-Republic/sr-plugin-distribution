# Brand Resource

Use the visible product name **StandUp Republic Internal** and the positioning
**SR Assistant**. The stable technical plugin ID remains `standup-republic` and
must never be changed to match display copy.

## Choose An Asset

| Need | Bundled official asset | Verified properties |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scalable wordmark on a light background | [Wordmark SVG](../assets/brand/standup-republic-wordmark.svg) | 220 × 112, transparent, paths use `#070707` |
| Wordmark on a dark or photographic background | [White wordmark PNG](../assets/brand/standup-republic-wordmark.png) | 157 × 80, transparent |
| Smaller white wordmark | [Compact white wordmark PNG](../assets/brand/standup-republic-wordmark-white.png) | 120 × 62, transparent |
| Square avatar, cover tile, or listing draft | [Square icon JPG](../assets/brand/standup-republic-icon.jpg) | 256 × 256, white background |
| Business Workspace Custom-MCP-App-Icon | [Workspace icon PNG](../../../assets/workspace-custom-mcp-icon.png) | 256 × 256, 4,021 bytes, decoded pixels match the official square source |

Prefer the SVG for documents when its black mark has sufficient contrast. Use
one of the white transparent PNGs only on a dark enough background. Use the
square icon when the destination requires a square image; do not stretch a
wordmark into a square.

Use the Workspace icon only when the Business Workspace Custom-MCP gate
requires a PNG of at least 256 × 256 and under 10,000 bytes. It is the
Listing-owned deterministic PNG derivative of the official square source.
Keep its provenance and gate metadata in the canonical
[Asset Manifest](../../../assets/manifest.json); do not copy either into this
skill.

Keep the original proportions, full star arc, and asset colors. The verified
primary mark color is `#070707`; the supplied alternate marks are white. No
evidence for recolored, cropped, redrawn, or otherwise modified variants is
bundled here, so reuse one of the files as provided. No canonical font or wider
color palette is bundled here; preserve the document's own typography and do
not invent additional brand rules.

The files are byte-identical copies of current Product-app assets. Provenance,
source commits, dimensions, descriptions, and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
the machine-readable [Brand Asset Manifest](../assets/brand/manifest.json).

The plugin card and composer use the Listing-owned
[square logo](../../../assets/logo.jpg) and
[composer icon](../../../assets/composer-icon.jpg). The canonical Listing
Asset Manifest cross-links this employee-document resource and records the
shared Product source. Do not reproduce Listing copy, screenshot briefs, or
Marketplace metadata inside this skill.
